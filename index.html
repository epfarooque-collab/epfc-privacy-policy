EPFC Privacy Policy

Privacy information for the EPFC cryptocurrency tracking app.

Effective date: 12 October 2026

1. Introduction

EPFC is a cryptocurrency tracking and market information application. This policy explains how information may be handled when you use EPFC.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features you use, EPFC may process account information such as your email address, preferences, watchlists, portfolio entries, and alert settings. If you contact us, we may receive the information you provide.

3. Market Information

EPFC may obtain cryptocurrency prices, charts, market statistics, and news from third-party providers. Those providers may process technical information according to their own privacy policies.

4. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to provide app features, save your preferences, display portfolio calculations, deliver requested alerts, maintain security, troubleshoot problems, and improve the service.

5. Storage and Security

We intend to use appropriate safeguards to protect information. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

6. Sharing

Information may be processed by service providers needed to operate the app, where required by law, or to protect users and the service. We do not intend to sell personal information.

7. Third-Party Services

EPFC may link to or use third-party services. Their handling of information is governed by their own policies and terms.

8. Your Choices

You can choose what information you enter into the app. If account features are provided, instructions for account or data deletion will be made available when applicable.

9. Children's Privacy

EPFC is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate authorization.

10. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as EPFC develops. The latest version will be published on this page with its effective date.
